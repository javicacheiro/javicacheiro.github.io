Welcome! I'm Javier Cacheiro López ([javicacheiro](https://github.com/javicacheiro)), Scientific
Director of the [OneHealth DataSpace](https://dataspace.cesga.es) at
[CESGA](https://www.cesga.es), the Galicia Supercomputing Center.

I've just rebuilt this site with [Astro](https://astro.build) and the
[Pure theme](https://github.com/cworld1/astro-theme-pure), and I plan to use it for two things:

1. As a landing page for anyone who wants to know who I am and what I do.
2. As a blog where I write about the technologies I work with every day.

## What to expect here

- **HPC** — supercomputing in practice: schedulers, storage, performance tuning.
- **Quantum Computing** — the road from experiments to useful quantum-HPC workflows.
- **AI** — training and serving models on real infrastructure.
- **Big Data** — data engineering at scale, from Hadoop's era to today's lakehouses.
- **Cloud** — automation and platforms for scientific and industrial workloads.
- **Data Spaces** — sovereign data sharing, the technology behind projects like the
  OneHealth DataSpace.

Stay tuned — and if any of these topics interest you, you can follow the [RSS feed](/rss.xml).
